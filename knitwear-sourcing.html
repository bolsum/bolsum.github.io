What we support

BOLSUM supports international buyers with Bangladesh knitwear sourcing from product brief and fabric selection through sampling, costing and production follow-up.

Product development

Review fabric type, composition, GSM, width, colour, hand feel, shrinkage and finishing requirements against the intended garment and target retail positioning.

Sourcing & suppliers

Coordinate sourcing conversations for jersey, rib, fleece, pique, waffle and other suitable knit constructions according to product specification and supplier capability.

Sampling

Plan sample development around measurements, stitch and seam construction, trims, artwork, wash or dye effects, and the fit comments that need to be checked.

Commercial control

Review how fabric consumption, GSM, usable width, garment construction, print or embroidery, wash, order quantity and colour count affect the quoted price.

Knitwear specification checklist

Details that shape a knitwear quote

Where available, include fibre composition, fabric construction, target GSM, usable width, colour count, size range, measurement chart, shrinkage or wash requirements, artwork, trims, labels and packaging. For fleece or other brushed fabrics, note the required surface and hand feel; for printed or dyed garments, identify the process and colour expectations.

From fabric to finished garment

Fabric and finishing choices can change consumption, appearance, hand feel, lead time and price. BOLSUM helps organise these variables for supplier review so quotations can be compared against the same product requirements.
