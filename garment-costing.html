Costing checklist

Compare like with like—not just the final price

A useful garment cost review needs a consistent specification: fabric composition and GSM, consumption or marker assumptions, size ratio, colour count, print or embroidery details, wash, trims, packaging, quantity and delivery terms. Clarify whether testing, sample charges, inspection, freight and other buyer-specific requirements are included or quoted separately.

What a costing review can clarify

Which components drive the price, which assumptions differ between quotations, what changes may affect the target cost, and what needs supplier confirmation. Any revised price remains subject to the final approved specification, quantity and supplier quotation.
