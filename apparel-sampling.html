What we support

BOLSUM coordinates apparel sampling in Bangladesh, including prototype development, fabric selection, construction, fit feedback and sample revisions.

Product development

Collect the reference, measurement chart, construction notes, fabric direction, artwork and trims. Flag missing details that could affect sample accuracy.

Sourcing & suppliers

Coordinate suitable fabric, colour, trim and decoration options for the requested prototype. Availability and minimums depend on supplier and material.

Sampling

Organise fit and workmanship feedback by priority—measurements, balance, seam quality, appearance, print placement and finishing—so revisions are actionable.

Commercial control

Agree the sample type, material assumptions, revision count, courier destination and target dates before work starts. Sample costs and timing vary by style.

Sample stages

Make each sample round useful

Proto sample

Check the overall design, construction and feasibility against the reference or tech pack.

Fit sample

Review measurements, garment balance and fit comments on the intended size or fit model.

Revision sample

Confirm that priority comments have been addressed and note any points still open.

Pre-production alignment

Confirm the approved materials, construction, artwork and measurement tolerances before bulk production, as required by the buyer.

Not every project needs every stage. Sample sequence, charges and lead times should be agreed with the supplier for the specific style.
