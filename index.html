01 — About BOLSUM

A sourcing partner,
built around detail.

Good sourcing is more than finding a factory. It is managing the decisions between an idea and a finished product.

BOLSUM works behind the scenes to turn concepts, tech packs and product ideas into commercially viable garments. We coordinate sourcing, product development, sampling, costing and production follow-up with a practical, detail-focused approach.

Our focus is to make the route from development to delivery clearer, more controlled and easier for our clients to manage.
