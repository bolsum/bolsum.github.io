## Follow-up cadence

After a genuine first contact:

- Day 14: close the loop politely

Use the exact scripts on `buyer-outreach.html`.

## Reply handling

When a prospect replies:
- **Send your company profile** → send the BOLSUM portfolio/profile and ask which product category they are developing.
- **We already have suppliers** → offer BOLSUM as an additional sourcing/development resource, without criticizing existing suppliers.
- **Send your prices** → ask for product, quantity, fabric/specification and target market before quoting.
- **Maybe later** → acknowledge and keep the door open.
- **Yes, let's discuss** → move to a specific product brief and call/meeting.
- **Not interested** → thank them and close politely.

## Qualification
Use:
- **Qualified** — active project, clear product need, decision-maker engagement.
- **Developing** — relevant prospect but timing/project is not yet clear.
- **Park** — no current fit or explicit no.
