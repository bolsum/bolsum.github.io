Target profile

Who to prospect first

Brand / retailer

Small to mid-sized apparel, fashion, lifestyle, Christian lifestyle, streetwear, activewear or casualwear businesses that develop physical products.

Geography

Start with UK, EU and US markets where Bangladesh apparel sourcing is commercially relevant.

Buying signal

Look for new collections, supplier expansion, new product launches, sourcing roles, product-development hiring or visible Bangladesh/Asia sourcing activity.

Decision makers

Search for these titles.

Founder / Owner

Best for smaller brands where buying decisions are founder-led.

Head / Director of Buying

Relevant for brands and retailers with established buying teams.

Sourcing / Product Development

Look for Sourcing Manager, Product Development Manager, Production Manager, Technical Manager and related roles.

Daily operating target

10 qualified prospects per working day.

Step 1Find 10 companies that fit the target profile. Do not add companies simply to hit the number.
Step 2Identify one relevant decision maker per company and record name, role, company, country and public profile/contact source.
Step 3Write one specific reason for contacting them: product category, collection, sourcing need, role or development signal.
Step 4Send the first contact. Keep the message short; do not send a generic company brochure in the first line.
Step 5Record the date and next follow-up. Never rely on memory.
LinkedIn connection

Short first contact.

Hi [Name] — I work in apparel sourcing and product development from Bangladesh and recently came across [Company]. I’d be glad to connect and share how BOLSUM supports product development, sourcing, sampling and production follow-up.
After connection

Start with relevance, not a sales dump.

Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I noticed [specific product / collection / sourcing signal] at [Company]. BOLSUM supports apparel product development and Bangladesh sourcing, including sampling, costing and supplier coordination. If you are developing new styles or reviewing suppliers, I’d be happy to discuss a specific product brief.
Follow-up

Three useful touches.

Day 3 Hi [Name], just following up in case Bangladesh sourcing or product development is relevant for any upcoming styles. Happy to review one product brief and advise on the development route. Day 7 Hi [Name], one practical way we can start is with a single style. You can send the reference/tech pack, quantity and target price, and I can review the sourcing and costing requirements. Day 14 Hi [Name], I’ll close the loop here for now. If you have a future apparel development or sourcing requirement, you can send the brief through https://bolsum.github.io/for-buyers.html and I’ll take a look.
Qualification

Do not chase every lead.

Qualified

Real apparel product, identifiable decision maker, plausible quantity, defined or developing timeline, and genuine sourcing/development need.

Developing

Good company fit but timing or product requirement is not yet clear. Keep in follow-up.

Park

No relevant product, no accessible decision maker, or no meaningful sourcing signal. Revisit later rather than repeatedly messaging.

Rule

Personalize the first sentence. Standardize the rest.

Never claim a buyer is looking for a new supplier unless you have evidence. Never invent production volumes, existing suppliers, certifications, customer names or sourcing activity. Use only information you can verify publicly or that the prospect gives you.
