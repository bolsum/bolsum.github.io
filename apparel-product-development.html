What we support

BOLSUM coordinates apparel product development from Bangladesh, translating product briefs and tech packs into practical samples, materials, costing and production-ready specifications.

Product development

Review the brief or tech pack for construction, measurements, fabric, trims, artwork, labelling and packaging. List missing information before supplier development begins.

Sourcing & suppliers

Coordinate material and trim options that fit the intended design, performance, target cost and production practicality. Final options depend on availability and supplier capability.

Sampling

Translate the approved brief into sample requests, track comments and organise revisions so the next sample addresses the buyer's priority points.

Commercial control

Consider fabric, construction, trims, decoration, washing, quantity and packaging together. Early commercial review helps flag cost drivers before bulk commitment.

Development stages

From concept to an approved specification

01 · Brief review

Collect references, measurements, fabric direction, colourways, artwork and target requirements.

02 · Material and construction

Clarify fabric, trims, seams, finishes and decoration options with the supplier.

03 · Sample and comments

Coordinate the sample, record fit or workmanship feedback and track required revisions.

04 · Commercial alignment

Review the updated specification, cost assumptions, MOQ and estimated timeline before order decisions.

Buyer approvals and final specifications remain the reference for production; timing and feasibility are confirmed for each project.
